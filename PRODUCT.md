# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **General Football Enthusiasts**: Fans who follow their favorite club or nation, but have extra time and want to discover additional thrilling games for entertainment without prejudice toward a specific league or country.
- **Multi-Stream & Night Viewers**: Fans with broad broadcast/streaming access (e.g., European competition nights like Champions League/Europa League, stacked weekend slates, international tournaments) needing an immediate, objective guide to choose which concurrent game will deliver the most excitement.

## Product Purpose

`findfootball.games` eliminates match-selection paralysis by scoring and ranking football fixtures based on pure entertainment potential. It helps fans discover high-intensity, nail-biting matches they might otherwise overlook.

## Positioning

An agnostic, data-driven match prioritization engine and competition visualizer that measures true entertainment value—surfacing high-stakes under-the-radar clashes alongside marquee derbies without league bias.

## Operating Context

- **Decision Moments**: Real-time / pre-match selection during crowded matchdays (e.g. 8 simultaneous UCL kickoff slots, Saturday multi-league windows).
- **Tournament & Competition Tracking**: Exploring tournament brackets, group stages, and qualification scenarios with interactive simulations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Dynamic Watchability Score**: Mathematical rating combining ELO proximity, betting odds competitiveness, team/player form streaks, and stage stakes, customizable with slider weights.
- **Fixture Surfacing ("Hot List")**: Highlighting games exceeding high-entertainment thresholds (75%+ score).
- **Rich Visualizations**: Interactive tournament bracket simulators (FIFA 48-team bipartite constraint solver), group tables, and calendar schedules.
- **Domain Constraint**: Strictly dedicated to association football across leagues, cups, and international competitions.

## Product Principles

1. **Entertainment-First & League-Agnostic**: Highlight the best football to watch regardless of prestige, division, or broadcast popularity.
2. **Instant Decision Clarity**: Deliver quick, clear signals and rankings so viewers can pick a match in seconds before kickoff.
3. **Transparent & Interactive Metrics**: Allow fans to see why a match scores high and tune the mathematical engine to their own watching preferences.
4. **Immersive Competition Context**: Pair individual match recommendations with clear, visual tournament dynamics and standings.

## Accessibility 

- Responsive layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- High contrast scores, clear badges, and accessible theme palettes.
